About UpSkillNow

Empowering professionals worldwide with industry-leading technology training and career advancement

Our Story

UpSkillNow was founded in 2018 with a simple yet powerful mission: to bridge the gap between traditional education and industry-required skills. We recognized that professionals needed practical, hands-on training in emerging technologies like Power BI, Data Analytics, and AI to stay competitive in today's rapidly evolving job market.

What started as a small training center has grown into a premier technology education platform serving over 5,000 students across multiple cities. Our success is built on a foundation of expert instructors, innovative curriculum, and an unwavering commitment to student success.

Today, UpSkillNow continues to be at the forefront of professional development, helping individuals and enterprises master cutting-edge technologies that drive business transformation.

Our Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To empower professionals and organizations with industry-ready technology skills through quality education, expert mentorship, and proven career support, enabling them to succeed in the digital economy.

🌟 Our Vision

To be the most trusted technology training academy in India, known for transforming careers and creating leaders who drive innovation in their organizations and industries.

Why Students Choose UpSkillNow

Industry-Relevant Curriculum

Our courses are designed in collaboration with industry leaders and updated quarterly to reflect current market demands. We focus on practical skills that employers actually need, not outdated theoretical concepts.

Expert Instructors

All our instructors bring 10+ years of real-world experience in their respective domains. They understand the challenges professionals face and teach with practical examples from their own careers.

Hands-On Learning Approach

We believe in learning by doing. Our 70:30 practical-to-theory ratio ensures students work on real-world projects, build portfolios, and gain confidence in applying their knowledge.

Placement Guarantee

We don't just teach; we ensure placement. Our dedicated placement cell works with 500+ companies to find opportunities for our graduates. 95% of our students secure relevant roles within 3 months of completion.

Flexible Learning Options

Whether you prefer live online classes, in-person batches, or self-paced learning, we have a flexible model that fits your schedule and learning style.

Post-Training Support

Our support doesn't end at course completion. Students receive lifetime access to materials, can attend refresher sessions, and have continued access to our vibrant community of professionals.
