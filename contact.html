Common Questions

How quickly will you respond?

We reply to all messages within 24 hours on business days. For urgent queries, call or WhatsApp us directly.

Can I visit the office before enrolling?

Absolutely! You're welcome Mon – Sat, 10 AM – 6 PM. We also offer free demo classes.

Do you offer EMI or payment plans?

Yes — 3, 6, and 12-month 0% EMI through partner banks. Ask our admissions team.

Is there a free trial class?

Yes! Every course has a free demo session. Contact us to register. No commitment needed.

Do you offer group or corporate discounts?

Groups of 3+ get 10% off. Corporate batches are priced separately — email corporate@upskillnow.com.

Can I switch courses after enrolment?

Yes, within the first week you may switch to any course of equal or higher value at no extra charge.
