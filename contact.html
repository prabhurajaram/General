Common Questions

How quickly will you respond?

We reply to all messages within 24 hours on business days. For urgent queries, call or WhatsApp us directly.

Can I visit the office before enrolling?

Absolutely! You're welcome to visit Mon – Sat, 10 AM – 6 PM. We also offer free demo classes so you can experience our teaching style.

Do you offer EMI or payment plans?

Yes, we offer 3, 6, and 12-month EMI options with 0% interest through partner banks. Ask our admission team for details.

Is there a free trial class?

Yes! Every course has a free demo session. Contact us to register for the next one. No commitment required.

Do you provide group or corporate discounts?

Yes — groups of 3+ get 10% off, and corporate batches are priced separately. Email corporate@upskillnow.com for a quote.

Can I switch courses after enrolment?

Yes, within the first week of joining, you may switch to another course of equal or higher value at no extra charge.
